## Question

The stored refined isotope free energies, written before the freeze, show F01 accuracy rising with temperature (153, 185, 210 of 212 headline rows at 0, 300 and 600 K) and all 28 mp-985293 rows changing to the F01 sign between 0 and 600 K. Decision 08 proposed a mechanism: zero-point energy weights a force-constant change by about 1/sqrt(k), so bend and libration count relatively more at 0 K, while the high-temperature limit weights the H force-constant trace, which the O-H stretch dominates. P09 turned this into four predeclared tests.

## Method

`decompose.py` gained `--T` (default 300 K) and `--out-dir` (default `results/`). Defaults are unchanged. At T = 0 the mode weight is the T → 0 limit E/4 and the direct free energy is the zero-point energy. All runs used the frozen model set (MACE, ORB, MatterSim, SevenNet) and the same pairs, meshes and cached force constants; scoring used only the 212 frozen headline rows (MACE, ORB, MatterSim) and their frozen geometry (x = change in mean O-H, rmax). Per H values divide by `H_per_atom`. Compensation C(T) = -ddF_perp/ddF_str. Pair orientation is struct_b minus struct_a of `results/screen_pairs_refined.csv`, which is not always the order in `pair_id`; the scoring script reads it from that file.

Regression gate: the 300 K rerun into `results/post_freeze/T300` reproduces the frozen `results/decompose_pairs.csv` in all 1314 rows with a maximum difference in ddF_total of 0 meV/atom (the file is byte identical), well inside the 1e-9 meV/atom tolerance. The frozen files are unchanged (`git diff --stat results/decompose_pairs.csv results/decompose_structures.csv results/F01_cv.json` is empty).

H3 checks the Teller-Redlich product rule: at every q of the decompose mesh (shifted, Gamma free), the sum over modes of ln(ω_D²/ω_H²) must equal 3 n_H ln(m_H/m_D). H4 reads the on-site 3×3 force-constant block of every H atom from the cached real-space force constants, sums the traces per cell, divides by the atoms per cell and compares b minus a with the stored pre-freeze ddF(2000 K). Since 1/m_D - 1/m_H < 0, the high-temperature limit predicts sign(ddF(2000 K)) = -sign(Δ Tr K_H).

## Results

The decomposition reproduces the stored re-scoring: 153 of 212 headline rows have the F01 sign at 0 K (MACE 44/68, ORB 54/73, MatterSim 55/71), 185 at 300 K and 210 at 600 K (67/68, 73/73, 70/71). The mp-985293 rows score 0, 8 and 28 of 28. All 212 headline rows pass the quadrature check (quadrature against direct free energy within 0.01 meV/atom per structure) at 0 and 300 K and 190 of 212 at 600 K, where omitted soft modes in the direct sum weigh more. Among the 28 mp-985293 rows, 24 are clean at 600 K (all 28 at 0 and 300 K; largest gap 0.012 meV/atom). Quadrature and direct ddF give the same sign in all 212 headline rows at 0, 300 and 600 K, so the counts 153, 185, 210 and the mp-985293 28 of 28 do not depend on the quadrature; the smallest \|ddF_direct\| among those 28 rows at 600 K is 0.134 meV/atom.

**H1, compensation falls by more than 40% from 0 to 600 K in at least 80% of rows: FAIL.** All 212 rows are eligible (|ddF_str| ≥ 0.1 meV/H at both temperatures). C(600 K) < 0.6 C(0 K) holds in 98 of 212 (46%): MACE 33 of 68, ORB 32 of 73, MatterSim 33 of 71. The direction is right almost everywhere, since C falls between 0 and 600 K in 198 of 212 rows, and the median C goes from 0.665 at 0 K to 0.582 at 300 K and 0.406 at 600 K. The size is not: the median ratio C(600 K)/C(0 K) is 0.613 (quartiles 0.559 and 0.688), so the typical drop is about 39%, not the factor of about 3 that the 1/sqrt(k) estimate suggested. The predeclared bar was not met and the causal clause of decision 08 action 9 is therefore not supported as stated.

**H2, the mp-985293 reversal sits in the bend and libration share: PASS, 28 of 28.** In every one of the 28 rows the stretch term has the F01 sign at 0 K and at 600 K, C(0 K) > 1 and C(600 K) < 1. The stretch term barely moves (it shrinks by 1 to 3% from 0 to 600 K), while C goes from 1.44 to 1.59 (MACE), 1.20 to 1.31 (ORB) and 1.11 to 1.22 (MatterSim) at 0 K to 0.65 to 0.90 at 600 K. The ratio C(600 K)/C(0 K) for these rows is 0.56 to 0.62, the same as the population median. mp-985293 does not decay faster than other structures; it starts with a larger compensation and so needs a higher temperature to cross 1.

**H3, product rule: PASS.** All 31 structure and model combinations in the 10 mp-985293 pairs satisfy the rule at every q with a maximum deviation of 2.6e-11 per H, against a tolerance of 1e-3. Over all 224 headline structure and model combinations the maximum is 2.3e-9 per H. No row is flagged by this test. The rule is a determinant identity of the mass-scaled dynamical matrix, so passing it shows that mass scaling and mode counts are intact on the mesh; it does not test the force field or the treatment of imaginary modes. The quadrature is covered separately by the direct-sign check above.

**H4, 2000 K signs against the trace-limit prediction: PASS after orientation fix, 211 of 212 (99.5%, bar 202).** MACE 67 of 68, ORB 73 of 73, MatterSim 71 of 71. With the stretch near 5000 K, ħω/kT is about 2.5 at 2000 K, so this is a sign test, not a check that 2000 K is asymptotic. The one disagreement (mp-754004__mp-768422, MACE) has Δ Tr K_H = -0.024 eV/Å² per atom and ddF(2000 K) = -0.008 meV/atom, both near zero. Not predeclared, for context: the phase with the shorter mean O-H has the larger H trace in all 212 rows, and the trace sign also matches the 600 K decomposition total in 210 of 212.

**Crossover temperatures (descriptive, not predeclared).** Interpolating the F01-signed ddF_total of each mp-985293 row linearly on a 25 K grid from 300 to 600 K: MACE crosses between 431 and 506 K in all 10 rows, ORB between 317 and 406 K in all 9, MatterSim below 300 K in 8 of 9 and at 318 K in mp-985293__JVASP-29795. "Below 300 K" means the crossing is not resolved: the grid starts at 300 K. This agrees with the stored 0, 300, 600 K grid in decision 08.

## Verdict

H1 fails, H2, H3 and H4 pass. What F09 supports: the F01 sign at 300 K is consistent with the classical trace-limit prediction at 2000 K (H4), the stronger hydrogen bond phase has the larger H trace in every headline row, and the mp-985293 counterexample is a crossover in temperature, not a mass-scaling or quadrature artefact (H3 and the direct-sign check) and not a stretch sign change (H2). What it does not support: the specific claim that zero-point weighting of bend and libration by 1/sqrt(k) explains the size of the change. Compensation does fall with temperature in 198 of 212 rows, but by about 39% at the median, which misses the predeclared 40% drop in 80% of rows. Under decision 08 action 9, H2 and H3 passing makes the first "after the freeze" sentence eligible for the 16:00 check; H1 failing rules out the causal clause. Decision 09 skipped the video sentence.

The 600 K values describe the harmonic isotope term at fixed 0 K geometry, not phase stability; gibbsite and many hydrates decompose below 600 K. The three potentials are separate models, not independent validations, and no polymorph isotope free energy has been measured to test any of this directly.

## Disclosure

The scoring script was run more than once before this file was written. The first run (22 Oct, about 10:34) gave H4 124 of 212 (fail) and implausible mp-985293 crossover temperatures. The cause was a pair orientation bug in `f09_temperature.py`, not in the hypothesis: the trace difference and the crossover curves took b minus a in `pair_id` order, while ddF and x in `decompose_pairs.csv` are struct_b minus struct_a of `results/screen_pairs_refined.csv`, and the two orders differ in 1756 of 3860 refined rows. A check after the fix confirms the frame: b minus a of the frozen per-structure mean O-H lengths reproduces the frozen x in 212 of 212 headline rows with the struct_a/struct_b order and in only 125 with the `pair_id` order. The script now reads the orientation from that file and asserts that its 300 K crossover value equals the 300 K decomposition total in every mp-985293 row. The hypothesis text, threshold and row set did not change, and H1, H2 and H3 gave the same verdicts in every run. An earlier version of this file, committed in dfacd79, attributed the change to the on-site force-constant lookup; that lookup was not changed. A later run only added descriptive fields to the H1 block of the JSON. A reader should still weigh H4 knowing it was scored after a code fix that a failing first result prompted.
