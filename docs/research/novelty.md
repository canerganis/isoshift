# Novelty check: isotope effects on polymorph stability, inorganic, high-throughput

Verdict: no systematic high-throughput screen of H/D isotope shifts of polymorph transition T or P across inorganic hydrogen compounds found (about 10 targeted web queries). Single-system work and one 115-hydride harmonic study exist, so the claim must be "first MLIP-based, cross-family map of transition shifts and of harmonic-validity limits", not "first isotope calculation". Residual risk is moderate: web search only, no Scholar or arXiv full-text search.

## Closest prior art (cite and differentiate)
- Bourgeois, Crivello, Cenedese, Paul-Boncour, Joubert, J. Phys.: Condens. Matter 2018. High-throughput harmonic phonons for 115 MHy hydrides (30 metals, y=0.5,1,2), H/D/T formation enthalpies and pressures, isotope inversion with T. https://hal.archives-ouvertes.fr/hal-02011296 . Difference: formation (gas-solid), not polymorph transition shifts, DFT not MLIP, metals only. Biggest threat.
- Pamuk, Allen, Fernandez-Serra, PRB 92, 134105 (2015). QHA ice XI/Ih H2O/D2O. https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02009 . Single system, our method in miniature.
- Chen et al., arXiv:1607.01970. LiH/LiD/LiT B1-B2 QHA phase diagram. Single system.
- Zhang and Wang, arXiv:2507.01452. MACE + neural canonical transformation for ice VIII to X, H2O only. https://arxiv.org/html/2507.01452v1 . MLIP + NQE for a transition but one compound and no isotope scan.
- Fallacara et al. J. Phys. Chem. C 2021 (KOH/KOD PIMD), Sano-Furukawa et al. Sci. Rep. 2018 (AlOOH/AlOOD neutron), Ackland et al. arXiv:2504.06244 (ice VII/VIII/X isotope model). System-specific.
- Errea et al. (PdH/PdD SSCHA), Dammak et al. (LiH QTB-MD), PdCuH2 MLIP anharmonic study (npj Comput. Mater. 2025, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41524-025-01553-1). Single hydrides.
- Molecular and organic isotopic polymorphism: ROY and acridine deuteration, 4-MPPCP (ORNL), Nature Commun. 2018 host-guest crystal (https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-018-02931-8). Experimental, organic.
- MLIP nuclear quantum effect work 2025/2026: MolCryst-MLIPs database (arXiv:2604.13897), NQE on peptides (arXiv:2512.05741), fine-tuned MACE-MP-0 for short H-bonded crystals with PIMD (arXiv:2606.24750). All molecular or single-system.
- Zero-point energy flips close polymorph rankings in glycine and in high-pressure hydrogen (search snippets). No isotope scan.
