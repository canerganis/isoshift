# Prior-art sweep: Elsevier / ScienceDirect side

Scope: does any Elsevier-published (or Elsevier-indexed) work already state the claim that full H->D substitution favours the polymorph with shorter O-H bonds in O-H...O solids, with a harmonic ddF of about 0.12 to 0.15 meV per H per mÅ of O-H length difference, and a stretch zero-point term about half cancelled by bend and libration?

Result: none found that states this rule, its numeric slope, the ~200-pair inorganic polymorph test, or the boundary where it fails. Several papers cover the same observable (deuteration changes O...O or N...N bond length and shifts a phase or polymorph outcome) for single families or single compounds. Those are listed below with threat levels. Some of the closest hits are not Elsevier (IUCrJ, ACS, Springer Nature) and are included because they bear directly on the claim.

Method limits: Crossref member 78 filter applied for Elsevier. Crossref and OpenAlex totals are fuzzy-match counts and are not meaningful as relevance measures. Abstracts for most Elsevier records were not available through Crossref, and ScienceDirect/linkinghub pages returned only a redirect to WebFetch, so several threat ratings rest on titles and search snippets. Those rows are marked.

Rating notes:

1. The rule as stated (ranking by ddF with a slope and a boundary) is not in any hit.
2. Falk et al. (ROY) and Miao et al. (O-H...O polymorphic transition) are the two papers that come closest to the direction and the observable. Both should be read in full before the claim is worded as novel.
3. The geometric isotope effect literature mostly reports O...O lengthening on deuteration (Shi 2018, Liang 2022, KDP-type crystals). The claim is framed on O-H length, so the sign convention needs to be stated explicitly against this literature.
4. Ichikawa 2000 and Gallagher 1959 are the Elsevier precedents for the bond-length isotope correlation. Cite them if the claim's bond-length part is kept.

## Already-known items seen in the sweep

ROY H/D exchange (Falk et al. 2018) is not on the known list and goes beyond Zhou-Kye-Harbison, because it is a direct single-compound case with a bond-length change tied to polymorph selection. The IsoShift Zenodo record (doi:10.5281/zenodo.23578411, own work, 2026) also appears in OpenAlex results. It is the user's own earlier disclosure, not third-party prior art, and it dates the claim's public appearance.

## Bottom line

None found for the claim as stated. Closest partial pre-emptions are the ROY H/D polymorph case (Falk et al. 2018, not Elsevier), the O-H...O H/D polymorphic transition paper (Miao et al. 2021, not Elsevier, abstract pending), and the Elsevier geometric isotope effect precedents (Ichikawa 2000, Gallagher 1959, Henning 1989 for hydroxides).
