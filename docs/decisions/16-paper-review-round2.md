## Decision

1. **Option chosen: Major revision needed.** The statistics, equations and headline numbers survive three independent checks. The paper is not ready for PRB or PRMaterials for five reasons. Four are text and provenance errors that existing files fix. One needs compute.
2. **Blocking, text (fix now, about 2.5 h):**
   * **B1. Weak side: "fails", not "reverses".** All 20 weak-side evaluations have $\ddF>0$, so a constant positive sign gets 20 of 20 there; 18 of 20 have $x<0$; 17 of the 18 misses sit at $r_{\max}$ 0.98 to 0.99 Å, within 10 mÅ of a post hoc boundary. A reversed bond-length dependence and an orientation offset cannot be separated in the frozen data. Decision 05 (line 58) banned "reversed law" for the same confound on the earlier 1/16 set; decision 15 reintroduced "reversal" as a sensitivity statement without resolving it. This decision supersedes decision 15 on that word.
   * **B2. Sensitivity to the bend and libration term is not disclosed, and the stretch-ratio sentence is wrong.** The fitted net slope changes sign at $1/C$ = 1.8 to 2.0, not 1.9 to 2.3. Scaling the perpendicular term by 1.1, 1.2 and 1.3 leaves 167, 155 and 140.
   * **B3. Benchmark quoted only at DFT geometries.** The screen uses model-relaxed geometries, where the errors are 1.4 to 1.9 meV per H (ZPE difference) against a median headline $|\ddF|$ of 2.37 meV per H.
   * **B4. Methods gives the wrong mesh.** Headline values, decomposition and temperature sweep use about $40/a$ points, even, 4 to 12 (decompose.py line 61, f09_temperature.py line 69). Methods says $60/a$, 4 to 16, which is the refinement mesh. The substance holds: at $60/a$ the headline is still 185 of 212 with 0 sign flips (Opus recompute, max change 0.054 meV per H).

## Why

* **Weak side, chair re-check.** OH, $r_{\max}<0.99$, $|x|\ge 5$ mÅ: 20 rows, 2 right, $y>0$ in 20, $x<0$ in 18, 17 of 18 misses at $r_{\max}\in[0.98,0.99)$, below 0.98 Å 2 of 3 right; MACE 0/11, MatterSim 1/6, ORB 1/3. Opus and Astra reproduced the same numbers independently. A sign rule that a constant sign beats 20 to 2 on those rows is a failure; calling it a reversal reads structure into the orientation of 18 pairs. The prospective weak rows (2 of 9 right; 1 of 6 with stored $y$ has $y>0$) mildly disfavour a pure offset but are too few to decide. "Fails" is true under both readings; "reverses" is true under one.
* **Sensitivity, chair re-check.** On the 212 headline rows, $y_k = (\Delta\Delta F_{\mathrm{str}} + k\,\Delta\Delta F_\perp)/n_H$: $k$ = 0.8, 0.9, 1.0, 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 1.5 gives 200, 195, 185, 167, 155, 140, 123 right. Dividing the stretch term by $f$ = 1.1, 1.22, 1.3, 1.5, 1.8, 2.0 gives 167, 150, 140, 123, 101, 91. All-row slope compensation $C$ = 0.543 (MACE), 0.547 (ORB), 0.493 (MatterSim), so $1/C$ = 1.84, 1.83, 2.03. The paper's "the rule's sign would change only at ratios of about 1.9 to 2.3" is true only of the fitted slope and is still numerically off; as written it reads as a robustness margin the row counts do not have. A uniform rescaling is a sensitivity, not an error estimate: an error common to both terms leaves every sign unchanged, and the covariance of model errors between the two terms is unknown (Astra). F04's one measured pair gives a bend to stretch shift ratio of 1.11 against a model median of 0.71 (docs/findings/F04-differential-spectroscopy.md line 26), from a broad stretch band, so it points the unfavourable way without bounding anything.
* **Benchmark, chair re-check** (results/benchmark/summary.csv, b_relaxed). ZPE-difference MAE: ORB 1.42, MatterSim 1.37, MACE 1.91 meV per H; 300 K free-energy MAE 0.99, 1.18, 1.44; stretch MAE 42.0, 52.1, 64.9 cm⁻¹ with mean bias -14.6, -20.6, -40.3 cm⁻¹ (model softer); 22 of 40 PBEsol references have imaginary modes. At DFT geometries the stretch MAE is 30.3, 38.7, 45.2 cm⁻¹; 27.7 is SevenNet, so "28 to 45" mixes in the fourth model. Argon's b_relaxed values for MatterSim (1.01) and ORB ZPE (1.38) do not match the file; Opus's do.
* **Mesh, chair re-check.** Both scripts build `min(12, max(4, 2*round(40/l/2)))`. Methods line 80 is wrong; Opus's 60/a recompute shows it does not matter for the headline. It is still a reproducibility error a referee would find.
* **Why not "Not publishable".** Every displayed equation checks out in sign, factor and units (three members); 35+ numbers reproduce; the prospective failure, the multiverse, the clustered intervals and the orientation-invariant null are reported honestly. The remaining defects are wording, disclosure and one missing control, not a wrong result.
* **Why not "Ready after minor fixes".** B1 to B4 change what the abstract claims or what a reader can reproduce, and B5 is the control any PRMaterials referee will ask for first, given that the 0 to 300 K gain (153 to 185) rides on modes below about 900 cm⁻¹, where universal MLIPs soften most.
* **Chair's own referee attack, beyond the members.** The stretch-ratio check in Sec. stretch is framed as support ("follow the Libowitzky slope ... ratios 0.91 to 0.92"). With the corrected range 1.00 to 1.22 the honest reading is that the experiment allows a stretch term up to 22% weaker than the model, which costs 35 of the 54-evaluation margin over baseline. The paper should say this in the same paragraph, not only in a limitation.
