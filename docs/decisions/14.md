## Why

* **The use that the data support is a boundary crossed in P or T, not routine sample triage.** Median headline effect is a few meV per H, far below kT (25.85 meV at 300 K). The only place it plausibly changes what an experimentalist does is a phase boundary, which is the AlOOH card: +1.13 GPa (ORB) and +1.33 GPa (MACE) at 300 K, exploratory sign (CARD-AlOOH.md line 5, decision 05 E).
* **The strongest honest motivation is a measurement, not our model.** data/isoshift/validation.csv row 27, re-read: δ-AlOOH P21nm to Pnnm disorder at 9.0 GPa, δ-AlOOD at 13.4 GPa, +4.4 GPa, Sano-Furukawa et al. Sci Rep 2018, doi 10.1038/s41598-018-33598-2. CARD-AlOOH.md line 20 already uses it exactly this way ("shows that isotope effects in this system are large"). It is the concrete reason a neutron group should care, and it is checkable.
* **Astra's physics objection is adopted as wording, not as exclusion.** The 4.4 GPa is a different transition driven by physics our calculation omits, so it cannot support the diaspore sign. The paragraph therefore says "physics our harmonic models omit", puts our number in a separate sentence with "the models" and "exploratory sign", and README says "a different transition from the diaspore boundary IsoShift models". With that, a judge reads motivation, not a claim to reproduce 4.4 GPa.
* **Astra's abstract sentence ("F01 supplies a hypothesis about isotope-induced polymorph preference to test") was not used.** It is safe but gives a materials judge no system, number or action, and adding it to the gallery card costs words without adding content the card lacks.
* **Why not cut "Built before vs during" or hold 700.** Commit f7ecafa restored that paragraph on a stated reason: the form has no separate disclosure field and judging covers event-time work only. Cutting it to meet a self-set cap would remove a disclosure judges need. The binding limit is rendered length, so a page-fit check replaces the word cap as the real gate.
* **Why not Expand.** A "why it matters" paragraph plus an atlas lookup would pitch F10 and the rule as tools, which F11 and F10 rule out, and decision 13 forbids it.
