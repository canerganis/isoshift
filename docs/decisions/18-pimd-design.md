## Decision

1. **Production method.** MACE medium-mpa-0 in float32 (positions, momenta, normal-mode transforms, the centroid virial and all statistics in float64; TF32 and autocast off). Plain ring polymer, P = 16, no ring-polymer contraction. BAOAB ("middle") splitting at dt = 0.25 fs, PILE-L, tau0 = 100 fs, gamma_k = 2 omega_k. Four Gauss-Legendre points in g. Per structure: harmonic P-bead initialisation, 1000 pre-equilibration steps at g = 1 (discarded), then 3000 steps per mass point with 600 discarded and 2400 kept, warm-started point to point. 13000 steps, 3.3 GPU h per 96-atom structure.
2. **Reference.** Delta_anh := dF_PIMD,P - dF_harm,samecell,P, with the analytic P-bead harmonic value (`harmonic_samecell(...)["dF_P_eV"]`) on the same commensurate q set and the same COM treatment. The infinite-P same-cell value is stored too. ddF_corr = ddF_harm(full mesh) + Delta_anh(b) - Delta_anh(a). For a harmonic PES this Delta_anh is zero at every P, so the 1.8 to 5.9 meV/H harmonic bead error at P = 16 cancels exactly; what is left is the anharmonic bead residual, bounded below.
3. **Pilot first, on mp-625152 (delta-AlOOH), P = 16 and P = 32**, then a second P = 32 check on the H1 structure with the largest 1D MACE scan residual (A4b). P32 runs are checks only; every production value, both members of every pair, is P = 16.
4. **Scoring.** E_pair = 1.96 sigma_stat,pair + B_pair (biases added linearly). Resolved: |ddF_corr| > E_pair. Inconclusive: E_pair > 1 meV/H or a failed gate. Not run is reported as not run. H1 needs pairs 1 to 3; the denominator stays 3.
5. **Priority.** Pilot and H1 structures (5) first, then the second P32 check, then pair 4, then pair 5. Hard cap 48 measured GPU h. Pair 5 is dropped first, then pair 4.
6. **The eleven amendments A1 to A11 below are committed to P12 as section 2a before any production job.** They replace the float64 requirement, the raw-dF P vs 2P rule and the undefined "combined error" of section 2.

### Amendments to P12 section 2 (made before production data)

* **A1 Precision.** float32 MACE replaces float64. Reasons: 0.057 s vs 0.41 to 0.49 s per bead-step (measured); float64 P = 64 is 5 weeks. The cached force constants and the full-mesh ddF_harm were themselves computed with float32 MACE (`isoshift/calculators.py` line 52, `default_dtype="float32"`), so float32 PIMD runs on the same PES as the harmonic reference. Check: control C2.
* **A2 Same-P reference.** Delta_anh as in Decision 2. The P vs 2P convergence rule applies to Delta_anh, not to raw dF_PIMD: raw dF moves by 1.3 to 4.2 meV/H between P = 16 and 32 in these cells (harmonic bead error 1.79 to 5.89 at P = 16, 0.47 to 1.65 at P = 32, chair rerun of cells.py), so the literal rule cannot pass at any affordable P.
* **A3 No contraction.** The Markland-Manolopoulos split (harmonic FC on all P beads, (P/n_c) sum [V_MACE - V_harm] on contracted beads, residual forces projected back to all beads, Astra round 1) is formally correct but loses the O-H anharmonic signal. Exact FEP on a Morse O-H stretch (108 THz, omega_e x_e = 82 cm^-1, finite O mass), P = 64, chair rerun: Delta_anh 1.206 +- 0.009 meV full, 0.918 at n_c = 16, 0.677 at n_c = 8, 0.358 at n_c = 4 (transfer matrix 1.216). n_c = 8 keeps 56 %. Plain P = 16 with same-P referencing misses by 0.023 to 0.052 meV (A4). Physical reason, chair estimate: at beta hbar omega = 17 about 39 % of the bead-centroid variance of an O-H stretch sits in ring-polymer modes k > 4, which an 8-bead contraction drops, and the cubic and quartic terms scale with that spread. pimd.py has no RPC path, so this also removes an untested-code risk.
* **A4 Beads.** P = 16. Anharmonic bead residual, 1D Morse O-H, Delta_anh(P) - Delta_anh(96), chair rerun of morse2.py: P = 12: -0.18, -0.17, -0.19; P = 16: -0.052, -0.027, -0.023; P = 32: +0.017, +0.025, +0.030 meV for omega_e x_e = 82, 150, 250 cm^-1 (raw harmonic bead error at P = 16 is about 5.7 meV). Pilot rule on mp-625152 with d = Delta_anh(32) - Delta_anh(16) and its error sigma_d:
  * P = 16 is **rejected** only if |d| > 0.3 meV/H and |d| > 1.96 sigma_d (resolved and above the original 0.3). Then branch F (below).
  * Otherwise P = 16 is accepted and every structure gets s_P = max(0.05, its A4b residual, |d| - sigma_d), taking the larger d of the two P32 checks.
  * Why not a 95 % equivalence gate: with sigma = 0.160 meV/H per structure at 2400 samples and 24 H, sigma_d = 0.227 and 1.96 sigma_d = 0.445 > 0.30, so Astra's |d| + 1.96 SE < 0.30 cannot pass even at d = 0, and buying sigma_d near 0.1 costs about 5x the pilot. The pilot is therefore a gross-failure detector near 0.45 meV/H; the fine bound comes from A4b. This limit is stated in the results.
  * The harmonic prescreen `bead_transfer_ok` is reported but is not a gate: the harmonic bead error is removed exactly by A2.
* **A4b Per-structure 1D scan bound (new).** For each of the 9 structures, float32 MACE energy scans of each symmetry-distinct H (41 points, -0.4 to +0.8 A along O-H, all other atoms fixed), plus a scan along the O...O axis wherever O...O < 2.7 A (proton-transfer coordinate, covers a double well). Exact 1D transfer-matrix residual at P = 16 against P = 128 (morse2.py is the template), weighted per H. This number enters s_P. It covers the stretch and the 1D transfer coordinate, not stretch-bend coupling.
* **A5 Time step.** dt = 0.25 fs with BAOAB, set explicitly in every run (the `RingPolymer` constructor default is "obabo"; the CLI default is "baoab"). Measured on 64 harmonic O-H diatomics at P = 16 against the exact P-bead value: -0.054 +- 0.049 (0.25 fs), -0.228 +- 0.035 (0.5 fs), -0.774 +- 0.026 (1.0 fs) meV/H. A dt^2 + dt^4 fit to the two resolved points gives -0.059 at 0.25 fs, so s_dt = max(0.06, C1 result). No 0.125 fs MACE run (Astra round 1): the dt bias is set by the stiffest physical modes, which C1 measures on the real crystal spectra on CPU.
* **A6 Sampling.** Start from the analytic harmonic P-bead distribution of the PIMD-cell Hessian, centroid included (classical amplitudes), with zero amplitude on the 3 translations and on imaginary modes. 1000 pre-equilibration steps at g = 1, discarded and recorded. 3000 steps per GL point, 20 % discarded (the CLI default `--equil 0.2`), 2400 kept. The step count is fixed. Two predeclared, sign-blind exceptions: (i) if tau_int at the first point of the mp-625152 P16 run exceeds 24 steps (n_eff < 50), every run uses 4000 steps per point (that first point is extended in place); (ii) a point whose two kept halves differ by more than 3 sigma_half gets one extension of 3000 steps; if it still fails, the structure fails the stationarity gate. No other extension.
* **A7 Quadrature.** Four GL points kept. Nodes g = 0.72770, 0.80395, 0.90343, 0.97968; dF = -sum c_j <K_H>_j / n_H with c = 0.13988, 0.23737, 0.21123, 0.10390 (chair recompute, matches Astra). With exact K(g) the 2-point rule is off by 7e-4 meV and 4 points by under 1e-4 (morse2.py rerun). Check C4 on all 9 spectra.
* **A8 Cache match.** Match on energy, natoms, supercell, n_displacements and volume_A3 within 1e-4. Already in `find_cache` (commit 1122c05); all 9 structures resolve to one cache dir each (chair check: mp-625054 to 735696b601e78c63f893, mp-626497 to 884e7b6cd589d1ad93f6).
* **A9 Imaginary modes (pairs 4 and 5).** mp-626497 (-0.62 THz) and mp-23940 (-0.91 THz) each have one imaginary heavy-atom mode on the commensurate grid (nu_D/nu_H 0.959, 0.987). The same-cell reference is computed both ways: mode dropped (current code) and mode folded to |nu| (as `decompose.py` line 76 does on the full mesh). Folded is primary, because then the classical limit of the reference equals the exact classical isotope effect of the PIMD by the Teller-Redlich product rule, and a mode at beta hbar |omega| about 0.1 to 0.15 is classical. s_imag = |folded - dropped| = 0.023 (mp-626497) and 0.009 (mp-23940) meV/H. This is called an allowance, not a bound (Astra is right that it bounds nothing about a changed basin). Structure-retention gate: mean centroid displacement along the imaginary eigenvector below 0.1 A and no change in the O-H...O assignment; otherwise that pair is "not assessable". H3 and H4 stay exploratory.
* **A10 Error and scoring.** Per structure, sigma from block averaging through the GL weights. Per pair, sigma_pair = sqrt(sigma_a^2 + sigma_b^2). B_pair = s_dt + s_f32 + s_quad + s_P,a + s_P,b + s_hess,a + s_hess,b + s_imag,a + s_imag,b, where s_dt, s_f32 and s_quad (0.01) are same-sign effects counted once per pair, s_hess is the measured Hessian-check difference for the three interpolated structures (mp-625054, JVASP-54682, JVASP-92228), zero for the others. E_pair = 1.96 sigma_pair + B_pair is the "combined error" of P12 sections 2 and 3. Resolved: |ddF_corr| > E_pair. Inconclusive: E_pair > 1 meV/H or any failed gate (C1, C2, stationarity, Hessian check, retention). H1 support: 3 of 3 resolved with the harmonic sign. H1 challenge: any of pairs 1 to 3 resolved with the opposite sign. Otherwise undecided, with counts, denominator 3. H2 per pair: supported if |Delta_anh(b) - Delta_anh(a)| + E_pair < half-threshold (2.515, 3.000, 1.535 meV/H), exceeded if |...| - E_pair > half-threshold, else unresolved. The covariance between pairs 1 and 3 through the shared mp-23807 is stated.
* **A11 Priority and branches.** Run order is the run table. Branch T (tau_int rule fires): drop pair 5, then pair 4, as the cap requires. Branch F (pilot rejects P = 16): rerun mp-23807, mp-625054, JVASP-54682 and JVASP-92228 at P = 32 (26.4 h) so that pairs 1 to 3 are scored at P = 32 with s_P = max(0.05, A4b at P = 32, |d|/2); pairs 4 and 5 are not run. Branch F is decided on the pilot alone, before any other structure is run.

## Why

* **float32.** The 8x speed is the only route to 9 structures in 2 days. The force constants that define the reference were float32, so float64 PIMD would be the less consistent choice. The real risk is a float32 bias in the centroid virial, which a paired snapshot test resolves far better than a short float64 trajectory: per-snapshot differences of the same configuration have almost no sampling noise, while a float64 trajectory at 3.6 s per step would carry sigma near 0.3 meV/H and could not certify anything (both members agree on this).
* **Plain P = 16 over contraction.** The two numbers that decide it were reproduced by the chair: same-P residual at P = 16 is 0.023 to 0.052 meV in 1D and does not grow with anharmonicity; n_c = 8 contraction at P = 64 is 0.53 meV short of 1.21. Astra withdrew contraction in round 2 on these numbers. Contraction works for slowly varying intermolecular forces; an O-H stretch residual is not slowly varying in imaginary time.
* **Pilot on mp-625152, not mp-625054 (Astra round 2 over Opus).** The harmonic bead error is cancelled exactly by A2, so the structure with the largest harmonic bead error (mp-625054) is not the hardest test. The hardest is the structure with the most anharmonic, shortest H bond, delta-AlOOH, which also carries the AlOOH prediction pair. Running the pilot first makes branch F cheap: if P = 16 fails, nothing else has been spent at P = 16.
* **Second check by A4b, not by name.** The rule picks the second P32 structure from a pre-PIMD model number, so it is predeclared and blind to the PIMD result. It replaces Opus's mp-625054 and Astra's JVASP-92228 with whichever H1 structure the MACE scans say is most at risk.
* **The pilot rule.** It keeps P12's 0.3 meV/H, now on Delta_anh, and only rejects P = 16 on a resolved difference. The |d| - sigma_d term carries the pilot's information into B_pair without demanding an equivalence test the budget cannot buy (1.96 sigma_d = 0.445 > 0.3).
* **Imaginary modes.** Dropping the mode leaves a known classical offset of 0.023 and 0.009 meV/H in the reference. Folding to |nu| removes it and matches the full-mesh treatment in decompose.py. Neither says anything about a basin change, which is why the retention gate exists and H3/H4 stay exploratory.
* **No 0.125 fs run and no float64 trajectories.** Together 8 to 12 GPU h for controls that either cannot resolve the tolerance (float64 trajectory) or measure something C1 measures on CPU (dt bias on the real spectra). That budget buys pair 4 and the second P32 check.
* **Sign and units.** dF = F_D - F_H = -sum c_j <K_H>_j / n_H < 0 (same-cell harmonic -77 to -93 meV/H in these cells); the classical part, -26.849 meV/H, is identical for both polymorphs and cancels in ddF; ddF > 0 means deuteration favours a.

## Where the members converged

* float32 MACE inference with float64 integration and accumulation, TF32 and autocast off (both, round 2).
* Delta_anh against the same-P analytic harmonic value, infinite-P value kept; the P vs 2P rule moves from raw dF to Delta_anh (both).
* No contraction; plain P = 16 (Astra withdrew P64/n_c8 in round 2 on the Morse numbers).
* BAOAB explicit at 0.25 fs, PILE-L as in P12, four GL points, CPU 4 vs 8 point check (both).
* 95 % interval plus linearly added numerical allowances as the combined error; missing or inconclusive pairs never count as support; denominator 3; H2 thresholds unchanged (both).
* Pairs 1 to 3 before exploratory pairs; fixed step counts, no sign-driven extension (both).
* Cache disambiguation by volume (now in code).

* **Pilot structure.** Opus: mp-625054, conditional mp-625152. Astra: mp-625152 and JVASP-92228. Adopted: mp-625152 first, second by the A4b rule.
* **Imaginary modes.** Astra: not assessable, no finite allowance. Opus: excluded mode plus kT|ln(nu_D/nu_H)|/n_H bound. Adopted: folded reference with the drop/fold difference as allowance, plus a retention gate; the pair can still be "not assessable".
* **Extra controls.** Astra wanted a 0.125 fs MACE run and float64 trajectories. Not adopted (Why). Astra's FeOOH transfer concern is covered by A4b on JVASP-54682 and JVASP-92228 and, if their scan residual is largest, by row 8.

## What would change this

* A4b gives any H1 structure a 1D residual above about 0.19 meV/H: that pair cannot meet the 1 meV/H bar at P = 16; run it at P = 32 using the row 8 slot, or accept it as inconclusive.
* The pilot resolves |d| > 0.3 meV/H: branch F (P = 32 for pairs 1 to 3, exploratory pairs dropped).
* C2 shows a float32 shift above 0.1 meV/H: float32 production is out and the council reconvenes; float64 cannot fit the budget.
* C1 fails (harmonic-backend PIMD off dF_P by more than 0.1 + 2 sigma): integrator, COM or estimator bug; no production until fixed.
* The smoke test is much slower than 0.057 s per bead-step (for example bead chunking overhead): pairs 5 and 4 drop in that order; nothing else changes.
* tau_int above 24 steps: branch T.
* A working, validated reference stronger than the harmonic FC (per-OH Morse) would make contraction worth testing again, but not inside this budget.
