# IsoShift: which polymorph does deuteration favour?

**Problem.** Neutron crystallographers deuterate samples to cut background; chemists use D2O or D2 to reach metastable phases. Both need to know when H to D moves the balance between polymorphs. The effect is a few meV per atom, below model static errors, so we compute only the isotope double difference, in which the static gap cancels. The organisers asked whether two days can reach a real discovery. We claim none; we show a two-day process that tests its own output.

**Method.** Force constants are mass-independent, so one phonon calculation per structure gives harmonic and quasi-harmonic free energies for H and D. An ensemble of universal ML potentials (MACE, ORB, MatterSim; SevenNet in sign votes only) runs over Materials Project and JARVIS-DFT polymorph pairs. {{SEVENN}} Measured volume isotope effects came first: brucite and LiH signs right in 4 of 4 models (Fig. 1).

**Result (F01, Fig. 4).** Across 197 hydrogen-compound polymorph pairs screened with three universal ML potentials, the harmonic H/D free-energy difference at 300 K changes by 0.12 to 0.15 meV per H for each mÅ difference in mean X-H length, a stretch term about half cancelled by bend and libration. When at least one polymorph has a mean model O-H length of 0.99 Å or more (a model-derived boundary for moderate to strong hydrogen bonds), deuteration favours the polymorph with the shorter O-H bonds in 123 of 130 O-H model evaluations with |Δ mean O-H| ≥ 5 mÅ (67 pairs, 23 formulas), against 82 of 130 for a constant-sign baseline fitted on leave-one-formula-out training folds. {{SEVENN}}

The 0.99 Å boundary is model-derived and was chosen after inspecting the data; re-choosing it inside each leave-one-formula-out training fold selects 0.99 Å in every fold and leaves the held-out count unchanged, which covers the threshold value but not the choice of regime variable. Without any threshold the rule holds in 124 of 146 O-H evaluations (85%). On all covalent pairs with a signal cut (|ΔΔF| > 0.1 meV per H) it holds in 129 of 150 evaluations (86%; 23 of 28 decided formulas). Below the boundary it fails (1 of 16 evaluations, 13 pairs, 7 formulas). In the O-H strong regime the fitted total slope is 0.140 to 0.151 meV per H per mÅ, with bend and libration cancelling 41 to 53% of the stretch slope. A structure-only version using mean O...O ≤ 2.92 Å is weaker (127 of 153, 83%, against 64%). The three potentials are separate models, not independent validations, and no polymorph isotope free energy has been measured to test the rule directly. {{SEVENN}}

**Prior art.** The stretch-bend competition behind the rule is established for hydrogen-bond isotope fractionation (McKenzie et al. 2014, 2015) and for competing quantum effects in hydrogen-bonded systems, and large-scale harmonic isotope thermodynamics has been reported for metal hydrides (Bourgeois et al. 2018). The contribution here is the systematic polymorph-stability version of that competition, its slope and its regime boundary. See also Li, Walker and Michaelides (2011), Zhou, Kye and Harbison on isotopomeric polymorphism, and the Ubbelohde geometric isotope effect.

**Tests with predictions committed before the data were read.**
*F02, volume isotope effects.* Prediction-first volume-isotope comparisons show mixed transferability: alpha-AlH3 has the correct sign with a 1.3 to 1.5x overestimated magnitude; GaOOH and beta-CrOOH disagree in sign with references of limited comparability. Earlier brucite and LiH checks reproduce the measured sign, while ice shows a large quantitative error. The misses lie in the strong-H-bond regime, where harmonic quasi-harmonic treatment omits Ubbelohde-type proton anharmonicity. These results do not identify a unique failure mechanism. F02 does not test F01: it passed where F01 has no skill and missed where F01 applies. α-CrOOH is not counted: H is disordered in the reference structure.
*F04, differential IR.* In one predeclared literature pair (goethite to ε-FeOOH) the O-H stretch falls and the assigned O-H bend rises, as predicted: nominal shifts are about -260 cm⁻¹ (from the midpoint of a 2600 to 3100 cm⁻¹ band, so anywhere from -511 to -11 cm⁻¹) against -273 predicted, and +290 against +195 cm⁻¹. The diaspore to δ-AlOOH pair was inconclusive. This is consistent with the sign of the compensating bend shift at the zone centre, not a measurement of its size or of the compensation fraction; the measured bend/stretch shift ratio (about 1.1) exceeds the model median (0.7).
*F05, mineral D/H fractionation.* All 6 scored signs and the order brucite > goethite > manganite are right in all three models; magnitudes are 5 to 10 times too large. Preset verdict: ordering only.
*Checks.* Fig. 5 (F03) compares model O-H stretches with measured spectra. F06: Codex reimplemented ddF_iso blind and matched ours to 1e-9 meV/atom on 9 of 9 rows; this checks code, not force fields.

**Cards (exploratory).** Exploratory harmonic/QHA prediction at 300 K: deuteration raises the diaspore to δ-AlOOH transition pressure by +1.13 GPa (ORB) and +1.33 GPa (MACE), using one linear fit through 10, 15 and 20 GPa for both isotopes (the MACE H root is extrapolated below 10 GPa), and by +0.3 to +0.4 GPa from 0 GPa geometries. The two models place the boundary 4.2 GPa apart but agree on the shift. The sign is the prediction; neither the size nor the sign is validated. δ-AlOOH undergoes isotope-dependent proton disorder in the same pressure window, which harmonic physics omits, and the boundary is crossed experimentally at high temperature, beyond the 300 K calculation. Goethite to ε-FeOOH, same status: +0.46 (MACE) and +0.40 GPa (ORB), roots extrapolated beyond the 3 to 9 GPa grid. Falsifier: side-by-side reversed-bracket runs on the H and D compounds under matched conditions. An opposite-sign shift contradicts the card; one not resolvable from zero is a null result.

**How the AI worked.** Valency Bond literature agents found the validation data; all 120 queries are logged (`logs/bond_queries.jsonl`). Single-pass extraction was unreliable (9 of 11 rows "ok", 2 survived checking), so a second reader and a board checked the numbers. Each formula passed a two-model mathcheck. Hard calls went to blind councils (Opus, Astra, Gemini) with decisions and dissent logged in `docs/decisions/`. F06 is an independent reimplementation.
