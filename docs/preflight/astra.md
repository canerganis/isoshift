**Main risk: precise-looking transition shifts from unstable phonons and an inadequate validation gate.** Review below treats feasibility numbers as reported, not independently reproduced.

“Before 17:00” means settle the policy, scope or access requirement; implementation can remain event work.

| Rank | Gap / risk — why it matters | Concrete fix | Before 17:00? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Imaginary modes invalidate current evidence.** Brucite −37 THz and AlOOH −1–2 THz cannot support quantitative harmonic free energies. `FixSymmetry` can preserve a saddle; sum-rule enforcement does not establish stability. | Quarantine affected results. Inspect unstable eigenvectors across the Brillouin zone; test unconstrained relaxation and convergence. Never silently discard or absolutize unstable modes. Match phase, temperature, pressure and isotope fraction. Without a credible transition reference, retain **exploratory** framing even if volume tests pass. [Ice study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23003032/). | **Yes: rewrite gate.** |
| **3** | **Static-energy cancellation is overstated.** Static ΔE cancels from ΔΔF at fixed conditions, but determines whether/where a crossing exists and therefore the relevant ΔS. Small ΔS can generate enormous, meaningless shifts. | Define phase/isotope signs; solve H and D crossings separately. Reject unbracketed, tangential or poorly conditioned crossings. Sweep plausible static-energy offsets; report ΔΔF alone when ΔT is unstable. | **Yes: method contract.** |

Keep 02:00 binding; target submission by 00:30. Record submission confirmation. [Official agenda](https://llmhackathon.github.io/agenda/). | **Yes: access and clarification.** |
| **5** | **Ensemble spread is not calibrated uncertainty.** Models may share training data and systematic errors; different supercell caps confound model disagreement. Selecting only agreeing models/pairs creates optimistic reporting. | Label bands “model spread,” not confidence intervals. Add numerical-convergence bounds and DFT residuals; retain all failures and audit some rejected pairs. Four agreeing predictions are not independent confirmations. [Documented MLIP bias](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41524-024-01500-6). | **Yes: reporting policy.** |
| **6** | **No numerical error budget for sub-meV signals.** Float32 differencing, displacement amplitude, supercell size, q-mesh and acoustic-mode treatment may exceed the isotope effect. Forced `'P'` also makes cell normalization especially important. | Require convergence of **ΔΔF**, not merely frequencies. Test primitive/conventional-cell invariance, equal-mass zero shift, H↔D sign reversal and classical-limit cancellation. Explicitly convert kJ/mol-cell, meV/atom and meV/H. [Phonopy units](https://phonopy.github.io/phonopy/setting-tags.html#thermal-properties-related-tags). | **Yes: acceptance criteria; execute before ranking.** |
| **7** | **QHA and pressure claims are underspecified.** One Hessian supports mass substitution at one geometry, not an entire QHA calculation. Boundary minima and structural changes between volumes can create false isotope effects. | Compute force constants at each volume; minimize \(E(V)+F_{\mathrm{vib}}(V,T)+PV\) separately for H/D. Require bracketed minima, stable branches and adequate internal/strain relaxation. Drop pressure-map claims unless explicitly implemented. [QHA definition](https://phonopy.github.io/phonopy/qha.html). | **Yes: scope and acceptance rules.** |
| **8** | **Throughput extrapolation is fragile.** Small-cell timings omit difficult relaxations, low-symmetry displacement counts, model loading, retries and sustained laptop throttling. SevenNet’s reported 7.1 GB leaves little headroom. | Start with a representative timed batch; estimate from its slow tail. Cache **unique structures**, batch by model, prioritize validation, impose runtime/VRAM limits. Scale toward 300 pairs only after measurement; reserve QHA initially for 3–5. | **Yes: budget/cut policy; benchmark early.** |
| **9** | **“Result file exists = done” is unsafe.** Partial JSON, stale parameters or duplicated workers can silently corrupt the overnight dataset. An hourly liveness check misses hangs and wastes substantial time. | Use atomic result publication, schema/status validation and hashes covering structure, model, settings and code. Add one GPU lock, heartbeat, job timeout, bounded retries and quarantine. Test crash, hang and stale-cache recovery. | **No: mandatory before unattended execution.** |
| **10** | **The fallback benchmark is not secured.** Ten–twenty downloadable, structurally matched H-compound references are assumed. Γ-point stretches alone do not validate full-zone free energies or low-frequency entropy. | First obtain and parse 3–5 complete reference datasets. Reweight DFT force constants for H/D; compare isotope free-energy corrections as well as stretches. Record functional, geometry, polar corrections and possible training overlap. | **Yes: verify availability; parity before gate.** |
| **11** | **Pair counts overstate usable candidates.** Formula/space-group labels are not unique structures; relaxations can merge nominal polymorphs. Cross-database energy differences may be incompatible, and near-degenerate phases may both be unstable to decomposition. | Deduplicate structurally before **and after** relaxation. Keep energy provenance; never subtract incompatible MP/JARVIS energies. Rank only distinct, stable branches within plausible temperature/pressure ranges; distinguish metastable pair crossings from equilibrium boundaries. | **Yes: eligibility rules.** |
| **12** | **The orchestration is itself a major project.** Overlapping ledger writes, shared-worktree commits, quota exhaustion and conductor-session loss can stop otherwise healthy computation. Multiple review boards consume the critical path. | Use one ledger writer and integrator, bounded module ownership, an external deterministic supervisor and a restart runbook. Timebox reviews; reserve quota for completion. One verified blocking defect must override reviewer majority. | **Yes: simplify architecture.** |
| **13** | **Negative controls cannot prove harmonic inadequacy.** Failure may come from MLIP error, wrong structures or numerical instability. Agreement may be accidental. Missing configurational entropy, tunnelling and anharmonicity are distinct mechanisms. | Predefine each control’s observable and excluded physics. Separate numerical failure, surrogate disagreement and documented approximation limits. Replace “provably cannot explain” with a bounded statement; never require controls to fail. | **Yes: claim policy.** |

Cite Bourgeois and existing benchmarks; describe search scope. Verify synthesis/deuteration routes only for finalists. [Existing phonon benchmark](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41524-025-01650-1). | **Yes: headline; evidence by Thursday afternoon.** |
